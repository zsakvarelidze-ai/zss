Where to save

Pick ZSS FILES\register once. Every finished outline is then written to geometry/own/<code>.geojson and the ones already there are drawn as neighbours. Without a folder, Save gives you a file to download.

Settlement

Green: register rows with a point. Amber: GeoNames places — their code is written at L9, parent + 0 for each skipped tier + n, and becomes a register row when the outline is saved. Orange: already outlined. Or click anywhere on the map with nothing selected and the outline is saved under the parent you type.

Outline

nothing selected

Click to add vertices, click the first vertex to close. Vertices snap to a neighbour's edge so two outlines never overlap or leave a sliver. Saved with the imagery name, zoom, date and initials; licence own, status asserted.
