Where to save

Pick ZSS FILES\register once. Every finished outline is then written to geometry/own/<code>.geojson and the ones already there are drawn as neighbours. Without a folder, Save gives you a file to download.

Settlement

Colours by level: regions, municipalities, Tbilisi districts, administrative units (A.U.), neighbourhoods, settlements. Each unit has a ■ square in its colour (regions from zoom 6, municipalities from 8, A.U.s from 10): tap it to select the unit and edit its line. A hollow ■ (from zoom 11) is an A.U. with no outline yet. An A.U. with an outline has its square in the middle of it; one without sits just above its centre village (the one with its name, else its first) — tap it, then Draw.

A.U.s one by one — untick a municipality to hide all its A.U.s, or one A.U. to hide just it (with its villages)

Green: register rows with a point. Amber: GeoNames places — their code is written at L9, parent + 0 for each skipped tier + n, and becomes a register row when the outline is saved. Pink: already outlined. Or click anywhere on the map with nothing selected and the outline is saved under the parent you type.

Dots

Add a dot: tap the map where the place is, give it a name and a kind — the code is filled in from where you tapped; drag the lime dot to fine-tune before saving. Move dots: zoom in and drag any dot, then Save. New and moved dots stay lime until they are in the register.
